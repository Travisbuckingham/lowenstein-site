Our Foundation

Guided by Enduring Values

Established to carry forward the philanthropic spirit of Lewis and Joan Lowenstein, the Foundation supports initiatives that create meaningful and lasting impact in the lives of others.

Education & Opportunity

The Lowensteins believed deeply in the transformative power of education. The Foundation honors this by supporting scholarships, academic programs, and initiatives that open doors for young people with potential to make a difference.

Community & Human Services

From healthcare and senior services to faith-based initiatives and community development, the Foundation invests in the fabric of Houston and beyond — strengthening the communities Lewis and Joan loved.

Integrity & Faith

Hard work, personal responsibility, and compassion were more than ideals — they were a way of life for the Lowensteins. The Foundation continues to support Jewish organizations and faith-based programs that reflect these principles.

Their Story

From Humble Beginnings to a Lasting Legacy

Lewis Lowenstein was born in Missouri and met Joan Shyer in Tennessee, where the two married in 1935. They later settled in Houston, where Lewis built a successful automobile finance business that eventually expanded across 10 states.

After retiring in 1960, Lewis devoted much of his time and energy to philanthropy, community support, and helping others. Joan passed away in 1998, and Lewis died in 2010 at the age of 100.

The Lowensteins believed strongly in hard work, personal responsibility, and compassion for others. They valued education, opportunity, and the importance of investing in people — particularly young people with the potential to make a difference in the world.

Helping Others Reach the Stars

Continuing the Legacy

Over the decades, the Foundation has quietly supported hundreds of organizations, families, and individuals across Houston and beyond. Guided by the values Lewis and Joan lived by — humility, generosity, and unwavering commitment to community — the Foundation continues to invest in the causes closest to their hearts.

Today, under the leadership of the Hidalgo family and a dedicated board, the Foundation carries this legacy forward — supporting education, healthcare, faith communities, and the well-being of those who depend on the strength of others.

Grant Guidelines

The following guidelines outline the Foundation's grant priorities, eligibility requirements, and application process. These guidelines may be updated periodically to reflect the Foundation's evolving priorities.

01

Mission Alignment

The Foundation supports organizations whose work aligns with our core areas of focus: education, healthcare, Jewish organizations, faith-based initiatives, senior services, and community development. Applicants should demonstrate a clear connection between their programs and the Foundation's mission.

02

Eligibility

Applicants must be recognized 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organizations in good standing. Priority is given to organizations serving the Greater Houston area and communities the Lowenstein family cared about deeply throughout their lives.

03

Funding Priorities

The Foundation prioritizes programs that create lasting impact — particularly those that invest in young people, strengthen families, support the elderly, and preserve religious and cultural heritage. General operating support, program funding, and capital projects are all considered on a case-by-case basis.

04

Application Process

When the Foundation is actively accepting applications, prospective grantees are asked to submit a letter of inquiry via email to the Foundation Administrator. Selected applicants may then be invited to submit a full proposal. All correspondence should be directed to Diane Guiberteau.

05

Review & Notification

The Board of Directors reviews all proposals at their discretion. Due to the volume of inquiries received, the Foundation is unable to respond individually to every request. Applicants selected for further consideration will be contacted directly.

06

Reporting

Grantees may be asked to provide brief progress reports and financial summaries. The Foundation values relationships built on trust, transparency, and a shared commitment to the mission of serving others.

Please Note: The Lewis & Joan Lowenstein Foundation is not currently accepting new grant applications. The guidelines above are provided for informational purposes. Please check back at a future date for updates on grant availability.
